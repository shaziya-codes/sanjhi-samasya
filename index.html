Background

Problems are known locally. Solutions stay local too — that's the gap.

Communities across Jharkhand run into everyday obstacles in education, healthcare, agriculture, water management, sanitation, environment and rural infrastructure. Most never leave the village or ward they occur in — there's no shared place to log them, no bridge to the students and researchers building relevant solutions, and no structured way for industry to back a fix once it's ready.

Sanjhi Samasya ("shared problem") closes that loop: a citizen posts a challenge, university teams pick it up as a project, and industry partners fund the pilot to take it from prototype to deployment.

The pipeline

From a complaint at the block office to a working fix in the field.

Four stages, each with an owner, so a challenge never stalls waiting on the "next person."

01 — Report

Post the challenge

A citizen or field officer logs the problem with location and details.

02 — Match

Route to a team

The platform surfaces it to relevant university departments by domain and distance.

03 — Build

Co-design a fix

Student and faculty teams design a solution; industry partners can fund it.

04 — Deploy

Close the loop

The pilot goes back to the community that reported it, outcomes tracked publicly.
